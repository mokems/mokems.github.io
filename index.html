Chengxiao Li

Hey! I am Chengxiao Li, a PhD student at AIoT Lab, CDS, HKU in Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Chenshu Wu. Before that, I received my B.Eng from Dalian University of Technology (DLUT) and my M.Sc from the University of Hong Kong (HKU).

Research interests

  • AI for healthcare, leveraging wireless sensors such as infrared array (IRA) and Wi-Fi for healthcare sensing tasks.
  • Wireless sensing, encompassing IRA, DToF sensor, mmWave radar, and WiFi applications.
  • Human-centric AI, especially edge-device-based healthcare and human–computer interaction.
